Privacy Policy

Skyscroll collects no personal data. Everything it works out stays on your iPhone.

Last updated 29 October 2026

Skyscroll is made by Vladyslav Nechytailo, an independent developer in Poland. Contact: nechytailo.vladyslav@gmail.com.

The short version

Skyscroll does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What Skyscroll uses, and where it stays

  • Screen Time access. With your permission, Skyscroll uses Apple's Screen Time framework to receive how much time you spend in the apps you choose. Apple shares those apps with Skyscroll only as private tokens: Skyscroll cannot see which apps they are, what you did in them, or what was on your screen.
  • What it stores. Time totals, the pace profile you pick for each app, your settings and the distances it calculates. These are stored only on your iPhone, shared between the Skyscroll app, its widget and its Screen Time extension.
  • Your device model. Used on the device to look up your screen's height so time can be shown as a distance. It is not sent anywhere.

Skyscroll makes no network requests. Nothing it stores is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and usage data is never used for advertising.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your iPhone may include Skyscroll's on-device data in that backup. That is handled by Apple under your Apple Account, not by Skyscroll.

Deleting your data

In Skyscroll, go to Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. You can turn off Screen Time access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Skyscroll does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Purchases

Skyscroll is currently free with no purchases. If optional purchases are added, they will be handled by Apple through the App Store, and this policy will be updated first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
