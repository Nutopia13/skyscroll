Common questions

How does Skyscroll get the distance?

It doesn't measure your scrolling. It takes the time Screen Time reports for the apps you choose, multiplies it by an assumed scrolling pace for the profile you picked (Short video, Feed, Long video or Light), and by the physical height of your iPhone's screen.

The number isn't changing

Check that Screen Time access is on for Skyscroll in the iPhone Settings app, and that you chose at least one app in Skyscroll → Settings. Open Skyscroll once after using your apps. iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so a widget can lag a few minutes behind.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap + in the top corner, find Skyscroll and pick a size. Customise it in Skyscroll → Widgets.

The widget still shows the old look

Remove the Skyscroll widget from your Home Screen and add it again.

How do I delete my data?

Skyscroll → Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.
