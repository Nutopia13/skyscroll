How tall is
your scrolling?

Skyscroll turns time in the apps you choose into a distance, then stands it next to something about as tall.

  • Home Screen widgetSmall and medium, with a pixel sky that follows the time of day.
  • Today or totalTap the widget to switch. Seven little bars show your week.
  • JourneyA log of everything you've passed, from a person to a tower and beyond.
  • Stays on your iPhoneNo account. No cloud. No ads. No tracking.

How it works

Skyscroll doesn't measure your scrolling. It uses the time Screen Time reports for the apps you pick, an assumed scrolling pace for each one (short video, feed, long video or light), and the height of your iPhone's screen. Skyscroll never sees what's on your screen.
